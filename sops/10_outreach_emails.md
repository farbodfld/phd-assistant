# First emails to supervisors

Purpose: sell Farbod. We are not asking whether he should apply; the aim is to make the
supervisor want him as a student.

## Must
- Specific to the project: name the project, show why his real work fits it.
- Confident, capable, enthusiastic. Not arrogant, never tentative or apologetic.
- Gap in background: acknowledge in one clause, then reframe with the closest real coursework
  or experience and his ability to pick up new material quickly.
- Close with forward momentum: ready to apply formally, CV attached, happy to send transcripts.
- Attach the master CV. Attach statement or proposal only when the advert asks for one.
- 150 to 250 words. Subject names the project.

## Never
- "Should I apply?", "Do you recommend I apply?", "Should I strengthen X first?"
- Re-using a generic paragraph across professors.
- Claims not in profile.yaml.

## Funding questions
If the advert's funding is ambiguous for international students (see 40_funding), add ONE short,
polite question near the end, e.g. whether the studentship covers the full international fee.
Tell Farbod in the Telegram preview that the question was added.
