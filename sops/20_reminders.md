# Reminder emails

- When: no reply 14 days after the first email (Reminder 1), and 14 days after Reminder 1
  (Reminder 2). After Reminder 2, stop and mark the row "no reply".
- Sent as a reply in that project's own Gmail thread, and about that project only. Two projects
  with the same supervisor get two separate reminders, each in its own thread.
- Short and neutral: ask only whether they had a chance to read the earlier email and whether
  there is any update.
- Do not re-pitch his background, do not say he is about to submit, do not offer calls or proposals.
- 2 to 4 sentences.
