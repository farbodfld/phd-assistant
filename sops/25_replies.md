# Replying to supervisors and admissions

Every email from a supervisor or admissions office goes to the strong model and gets:
1. A category: positive, rejection, question, request_docs, admin, other.
2. Insights: anything that changes how we treat this position, department or university
   (funding rules, "apply via portal only", "try X first", deadlines). Saved with scope.
3. Tasks for Farbod when only he can act (e.g. "try ESBMC examples before applying").
4. A draft reply when a reply is expected, following 10_outreach tone. Thank-you notes after
   rejections stay short and may ask about other funded positions in the department.
