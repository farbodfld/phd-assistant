# Scoring positions (0 to 100)

Score fit against profile.yaml only (real projects, thesis topics, skills, experience).
Weights, in order:
1. Direct topical overlap with his real work: AI agents and LLMs, recommender systems,
   semi-supervised anomaly detection, computer vision, predictive modelling.
2. Methodological overlap in a different domain (deep learning on medical images scores moderately).
3. Fit with production software engineering.
Unrelated fields (astrophysics, photonics, pure maths, wet lab) score low even with "AI" in the title.
Colour key: green 70+, yellow 50 to 69, orange 35 to 49, red below 35.
Priority to act: supervisors who replied positively first, then nearest deadlines.
