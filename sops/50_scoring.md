# Scoring positions (0 to 100)

Most PhD projects are interdisciplinary. What matters is the role Farbod would play in the
project, not the name of its discipline.

## Relevant (topic_relevant)
A project is relevant when a substantial part of the work is computational: machine learning,
AI, data science or analytics, computer vision, LLMs and agents, modelling and simulation in
code, or software, and the advert would welcome someone with a computing or data background.
This holds in any field: aviation fuels, ecology, medicine, climate, engineering.
Not relevant only when the project has no meaningful computational part (pure wet lab,
fieldwork only, purely theoretical) or requires a degree Farbod does not have (for example a
chemistry or physics degree stated as essential).

## Score = three parts, added
1. computing_share (0 to 40): how much of the PhD is computational work.
   40 the computing is the core of the project; 25 about half; 10 a supporting tool.
2. skills_fit (0 to 40): how well his verified skills and real projects match that
   computational work (Python, ML and deep learning, computer vision, LLM agents, recommender
   systems, anomaly detection, data pipelines, production software).
3. topic_proximity (0 to 20): how close the subject is to his past work (thesis topics and
   projects). An unfamiliar domain is fine and simply scores lower here.
Use only verified facts. There is no minimum score: every relevant, funded position is tracked.
Colour key in the tracker: green 70+, yellow 50 to 69, orange 35 to 49, red below 35.
Priority to act: supervisors who replied positively first, then nearest deadlines.
