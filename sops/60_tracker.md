# Tracker conventions

- Rows sorted by score, Rank renumbered after every change.
- Closed, rejected or not-for-Farbod rows are greyed (not deleted), reason first in Status.
- Status updates are prepended with a date; older history stays after " | ".
- Apply Method: Email (contact supervisor only), Portal, Email + Portal (email first, then portal).
- Email/Portal columns ticked when done; Email Sent, Reminder 1, Reminder 2, Portal Submitted dates.
